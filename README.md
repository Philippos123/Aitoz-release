# Sivell

> Less mental load. More meaningful work.

## What is Sivell?

Sivell is an AI-powered personal work assistant designed to help people manage the complexity of modern working life.

Today's work is scattered across emails, calendars, meetings, calls, tasks and applications. Keeping everything connected requires constant attention, context switching and mental effort.

Sivell aims to change that.

Instead of forcing people to manage yet another tool, Sivell acts as an intelligent layer across their existing workflow — helping them understand what needs attention, organize their work and take action with less friction.

**The goal is simple: less time managing work, more time doing meaningful work.**

## The problem

Modern work creates a constant stream of demands.

- Emails arrive faster than they can be processed.
- Meetings fragment the workday.
- Tasks accumulate without clear priorities.
- Important information gets lost between applications.
- People spend valuable time coordinating work instead of completing it.
- The mental load continues even after the workday ends.

Most productivity tools help people manage individual parts of this problem. Sivell is built around the bigger picture: helping people manage the workday as a whole.

## The vision

Sivell should feel less like another application and more like a capable assistant that understands your working context.

You should be able to ask for what you need in natural language, receive useful answers based on relevant information and delegate routine actions when appropriate.

Over time, Sivell should help users move from constantly reacting to incoming demands toward working with greater clarity, focus and control.

## Core capabilities

### One assistant across your workflow

Interact with your work through a single, accessible AI interface instead of navigating between multiple applications for every small task.

### Email and communication

Understand incoming messages, identify what requires attention, summarize conversations and help prepare responses.

### Calendar and meetings

Understand your schedule, prepare for meetings, summarize discussions and help turn decisions into actionable tasks.

### Intelligent prioritization

Bring important tasks to the surface based on deadlines, commitments and workload. Help users distinguish what is urgent from what is genuinely important.

### Workload awareness

Identify patterns in meetings, communications and outstanding tasks that may indicate an overloaded workday.

Sivell should help users create realistic working blocks, protect time for focused work and recognize when their workload needs attention — without becoming another source of pressure.

### Natural-language actions

Let users interact with their work using straightforward requests rather than navigating complex menus and workflows.

For actions that affect calendars, communications or other important data, users should remain in control through clear permissions and appropriate confirmations.

## Design principles

**1. Simplicity first**

The interface should feel calm, intuitive and focused. Complexity belongs behind the scenes, not in front of the user.

**2. Human control**

AI should support decisions and handle routine work without taking away the user's ability to review, approve and override actions.

**3. Context over complexity**

Sivell should connect relevant information across a person's workflow instead of requiring them to manually assemble the bigger picture.

**4. Privacy by design**

Access to emails, calendars, meeting content and other sensitive information must be carefully controlled. Data access should be limited to what is necessary, with transparent permissions and secure handling.

**5. Reduce cognitive load**

Every feature should justify itself by saving time, reducing friction or making work easier to understand. More notifications and more dashboards are not the goal.

## Desktop-first experience

Sivell is being developed as a desktop application using Tauri, with the potential to provide an accessible AI layer over the tools people already use.

The intended experience is fast and unobtrusive: invoke the assistant, ask a question, get help or complete an action, then return to the work at hand.

The desktop client is the interface. The broader product vision is an assistant that connects information, understands context and helps people act.

## Who is Sivell for?

Sivell is intended for professionals who spend much of their working day at a computer and coordinate work across multiple applications.

This includes managers, consultants, administrators, entrepreneurs and other knowledge workers who deal with a high volume of information, communication and decisions.

The focus is not on adding more productivity metrics. It is on making the working day easier to navigate.

## Product philosophy

Sivell is not intended to replace human judgment or turn every moment into an opportunity to optimize productivity.

It exists to help people regain control over their attention, reduce unnecessary administrative work and make space for the work that actually matters.

**Technology should carry more of the workload so people can carry less of it mentally.**

## Project status

Sivell is under development. Its capabilities, integrations, architecture and product direction will evolve as the application is built and tested.

This document describes the intended product vision, not a guarantee that every capability is currently implemented.
